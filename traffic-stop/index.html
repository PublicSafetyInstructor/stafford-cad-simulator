How to run the exercise

This simulator is the officer's cue sheet, not a substitute for radio practice. Read the observation, make your initial radio call, perform the simulated driver contact, request the checks over your classroom radio, then reveal the dispatch return. Complete the disposition and evaluation with your instructor.

No live DMV, NCIC, or law-enforcement systems are accessed. All identities, plate numbers, warrants, and records are fictional. Instructor supervises any simulated enforcement or arrest actions.
STAGE 1 · INITIAL OBSERVATION
SCENARIO

Vehicle stop

📻 Officer task: Initiate your traffic stop over the actual practice radio. Include unit identification, location, vehicle description, plate (using phonetics), reason for the stop, and occupants as appropriate.

STAGE 2 · DRIVER CONTACT

Documents & observations

📻 Officer task: Role-play contact, obtain identifying information, return to the cruiser, and request the appropriate simulated 10-27, 10-28 and 10-99 checks. Give the driver's information clearly over the radio. Do not reveal the return before making the request.

STAGE 3 · DISPATCH RETURN

Simulated records response

📻 Officer task: Acknowledge dispatch, explain your legal and safety considerations, and decide on an appropriate disposition. Some warrant hits require separate confirmation before action.

STAGE 4 · INSTRUCTOR DEBRIEF

Scenario disposition

Radio & professional practice checklist

Training note: A database hit, expired registration, or license status alone does not establish that a custodial arrest is appropriate. Determine the applicable law, verify information, follow agency policy, and use instructor guidance. No force tactics are taught here.
